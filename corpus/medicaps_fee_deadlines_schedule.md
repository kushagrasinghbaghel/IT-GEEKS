# MEDI-CAPS UNIVERSITY, INDORE
## COMPREHENSIVE FEE SCHEDULE, PAYMENT TIMELINES & REFUND POLICY (2024-2025)

**Effective Date:** July 1, 2024

### SECTION 1: SEMESTER TUITION FEE DEADLINES AND LATE PAYMENT SURCHARGES

#### § 1.1 Payment Schedule and Mode of Remittance
1. All enrolled students must remit their respective semester tuition fees online via the Medi-Caps ERP Payment Gateway (Net Banking, UPI, Debit/Credit Card) or through Bank Challan at the on-campus Union Bank of India branch.
2. Cash transactions or post-dated cheques are strictly not accepted.
3. The official semester tuition fee deadlines for the Academic Year 2024-2025 are established as follows:

| Academic Term | Standard Due Date (Without Fine) | Grace Period Deadline (Rs. 50/day) | Extended Deadline (Rs. 2,000 flat fine) | Portal Freeze & De-Registration Date |
|:--------------|:---------------------------------|:-----------------------------------|:----------------------------------------|:--------------------------------------|
| Odd Semester (Sem I, III, V, VII) | July 15, 2024 | July 31, 2024 | August 15, 2024 | August 20, 2024 |
| Even Semester (Sem II, IV, VI, VIII)| January 5, 2025 | January 20, 2025 | February 5, 2025 | February 10, 2025 |

4. If a student fails to clear all outstanding tuition dues by the Portal Freeze Date, their ERP portal access, attendance tracking, and library borrowing privileges shall be automatically suspended until dues are cleared along with a restoration fee of Rs. 3,000.

### SECTION 2: HOSTEL ACCOMMODATION, TRANSPORT, AND REFUND MATRIX
*(PLANTED CONTRADICTION CLAUSE 2.4)*

#### § 2.1 Residential Fee Schedule
1. **Annual Residential Accommodation Charges (2024-2025):**
   - Triple Sharing Room (Non-AC): Rs. 85,000 per academic year.
   - Twin Sharing Room (Non-AC): Rs. 100,000 per academic year.
   - Twin Sharing Room (Air-Cooled): Rs. 115,000 per academic year.
   - Annual Mess Dining Charges: Rs. 55,000 per academic year.
   - Refundable Residential Caution Deposit: Rs. 10,000 (one-time payable upon initial admission).

#### § 2.2 University Bus Transportation Charges
1. Medi-Caps University operates an extensive fleet of dedicated air-conditioned and non-AC buses covering all major sectors of Indore, Dewas, Mhow, and Pithampur.
2. Annual Transport Fee Slabs:
   - Indore City Routes (Zone 1 - Bhanwarkuan, Vijay Nagar, Palasia, Annapurna): Rs. 24,000 per annum.
   - Extended Indore Suburbs (Zone 2 - Nipania, Bypass, Rau, Rajendra Nagar): Rs. 20,000 per annum.
   - Outstation Routes (Zone 3 - Dewas, Mhow, Pithampur Industrial Area): Rs. 28,000 per annum.
3. Transport passes are non-transferable and must be carried during all transit commutes.

#### § 2.3 General Tuition Fee Refund Schedule (Upon University Admission Withdrawal)
Refund of academic tuition fee upon formal withdrawal from a degree programme conforms to the UGC Statutory Notification on Refund of Fees:

| Withdrawal Application Submission Timing | Percentage of Refund of Aggregate Tuition Fees |
|:-----------------------------------------|:-----------------------------------------------|
| 15 days or more before the formally-notified last date of admission | 100% (minus maximum processing fee of Rs. 1,000) |
| Less than 15 days before the formally-notified last date of admission | 90% refund |
| Within 15 days after the formally-notified last date of admission | 80% refund |
| Between 16 and 30 days after the formally-notified last date of admission | 50% refund |
| More than 30 days after the formally-notified last date of admission | 0% (No refund admissible) |

#### § 2.4 Hostel Accommodation Fee Refund Policy (PLANTED CONTRADICTION 3)
1. **Strict Non-Refundability of Hostel Accommodation Fees:** Notwithstanding any general university admission refund policy or statements contained in residential handbooks, **all hostel room accommodation fees, mess infrastructure fees, and amenities charges are 100% strictly non-refundable once the room allotment order is generated and the room key is issued to the resident**.
2. **Zero Refund Regardless of Departure Timing:** Under no circumstances shall an 80%, 50%, or partial refund be issued if a resident vacates the hostel within 15 days, 30 days, or at any subsequent date during the academic semester. The university reserves the bed space for the full academic year, incurring fixed overheads.
3. **Caution Deposit Terms:** Only the residential security caution deposit of Rs. 10,000 is eligible for refund upon vacation, subject to a mandatory non-refundable administrative processing deduction of Rs. 2,000, leaving a maximum refundable balance of Rs. 8,000.
