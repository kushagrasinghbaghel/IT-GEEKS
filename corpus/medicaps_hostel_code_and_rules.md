# MEDI-CAPS UNIVERSITY, INDORE
## HOSTEL CODE OF CONDUCT & RESIDENTIAL ACCOMMODATION REGULATIONS (2024-2025)
**Document Reference:** MCU/SW/HOSTEL/2024/03  
**Applicability:** All On-Campus Boys' and Girls' Residential Blocks (Narmada, Shipra, Betwa, and Tapti Hostels)  
**Effective Date:** July 1, 2024  

---

### SECTION 1: GENERAL ADMISSION AND ALLOTMENT POLICIES

#### § 1.1 Eligibility and Allotment Priority
1. Admission to Medi-Caps University hostels is a privilege, not a legal right, and is granted subject to availability of accommodation and strict adherence to university disciplinary conduct.
2. Preference in room allocation shall be accorded in the following order of priority:
   - First-year undergraduate and postgraduate outstation students whose permanent domicile is located more than 60 kilometers outside Indore municipal limits.
   - Senior meritorious students with a minimum CGPA of 7.50 without any record of hostel or campus disciplinary reprimand.
   - International, NRI, and national sports scholarship holders.
3. Rooms are allotted on a twin-sharing or triple-sharing basis by the Chief Warden. Single room occupancy is strictly restricted to final-year research scholars or specific medical requirements endorsed by the University Medical Officer.
4. Mutual swapping or unauthorized change of rooms without prior written permission from the Chief Warden is strictly prohibited and attracts a fine of Rs. 3,000 per resident.

#### § 1.2 Tenure of Residence and Re-Allotment
1. Hostel accommodation is allotted strictly for a single academic year consisting of two standard semesters (Autumn and Spring terms).
2. All residents must completely vacate their rooms within forty-eight (48) hours of their final semester examination, returning furniture, keys, and room assets in original condition.
3. Residence during summer break or semester break is permissible only for students officially enrolled in approved summer internships, research projects, or remedial academic programmes, subject to separate per-day charges of Rs. 450 (excluding mess).

---

### SECTION 2: TIMINGS, BIOMETRIC CURFEW AND LEAVE REGULATIONS

#### § 2.1 Campus Gate and Hostel Entry Timings
1. The entry gates for all residential blocks close strictly at 9:00 PM for girls' hostels and 9:30 PM for boys' hostels during weekdays (Monday to Saturday). On Sundays and official university holidays, entry deadline is 10:00 PM for both.
2. Every resident student must register their arrival using the facial recognition biometric turnstile installed at the hostel reception.
3. Any student entering the hostel after the designated curfew hour without prior written permission from the Warden shall receive a formal late-entry strike:
   - First occurrence: Recorded written warning and SMS alert to registered parent/guardian.
   - Second occurrence: Fine of Rs. 1,000 and mandatory counseling session with the Dean of Student Welfare.
   - Third occurrence: Suspension of hostel residency for fifteen (15) days with immediate campus de-boarding.

#### § 2.2 Night Outpass and Home Leave Modalities
1. Residents intending to stay overnight outside the hostel campus must apply for an electronic Night Outpass via the Medi-Caps Hostel Mobile Portal at least 24 hours prior to departure.
2. Night Outpass approval requires automated OTP verification or telephonic confirmation from the student's registered parent/guardian.
3. Unsanctioned absence from the hostel overnight without an approved Outpass is treated as a severe disciplinary infraction, incurring an immediate penalty of Rs. 2,500 and possible permanent hostel expulsion.

---

### SECTION 3: SPORTS AND EXTRA-CURRICULAR PARTICIPATION CONCESSIONS

#### § 3.1 Hostel Representation in University Sports
1. Residents participating in inter-hostel sports tournaments, university athletics, and cultural leagues must coordinate schedules through the resident sports secretary.
2. Practice sessions extending past standard gate timings must be pre-authorized by the Director of Physical Education and countersigned by the Chief Warden.

#### § 3.2 Sports Concession and Attendance Rule Reference (CONTRADICTION REFERENCE)
1. In recognition of athletic rigor and training travel, bona fide student-athletes who represent Medi-Caps University in Association of Indian Universities (AIU) West Zone or All-India National Tournaments are eligible for academic and residential attendance relaxations.
2. Under this residential-sports welfare protocol, the minimum classroom attendance threshold for qualifying athletes certified by the Director of Physical Education shall be deemed satisfactory at 60.0% of delivered contact hours. *(Note: This directly conflicts with the strict non-waivable 75% threshold in Academic Regulation § 4.2).*

---

### SECTION 4: ELECTRICAL APPLIANCES, UTILITIES AND ROOM MAINTENANCE

#### § 4.1 Permitted and Prohibited Appliances
1. Each hostel room is furnished with ceiling fans, tube lights, study desks, chairs, wardrobes, and single cots.
2. **Permitted Electronic Devices:** Students are permitted to use laptops, mobile phones, tablet computers, desk lamps, and personal grooming trimmers.
3. **Strictly Prohibited Electrical Appliances:** The possession or operation of high-wattage electrical devices including immersion water heaters, electric kettles, hot plates, induction cookers, room heaters, iron presses, and personal mini-refrigerators is strictly forbidden.
4. The hostel inspection committee conducts unannounced periodic room inspections. Confiscated appliances shall not be returned, and a spot penalty of Rs. 2,000 shall be levied upon each occupant of the room.

#### § 4.2 Energy Conservation and Cleanliness
1. Residents must ensure that lights, fans, and water taps are turned off before exiting the room. Leaving appliances running unattended carries a fine of Rs. 200 per occurrence.
2. Defacing walls, driving nails into plaster, pasting unauthorized political or commercial posters, or tampering with university property shall result in repair cost recovery from the caution deposit plus a 100% punitive surcharge.

---

### SECTION 5: MESS, DINING, AND HYGIENE POLICIES

#### § 5.1 Dining Schedule and Meal Protocols
1. The hostel mess provides four meals daily:
   - **Breakfast:** 7:30 AM – 9:00 AM
   - **Lunch:** 12:30 PM – 2:15 PM
   - **Evening Snacks & Tea:** 5:00 PM – 6:15 PM
   - **Dinner:** 7:45 PM – 9:30 PM
2. Meals must be consumed strictly inside the designated dining hall. Carrying mess utensils, plates, glasses, or food trays into hostel rooms or corridors is strictly prohibited, except for bed-ridden sick students upon written recommendation of the university physician.
3. Outside food delivery partners (e.g., Zomato, Swiggy) are allowed strictly up to the University Main Security Gate (Gate 1) and are barred from entering residential hostel blocks.

#### § 5.2 Mess Rebate Criteria
1. Mess rebate of Rs. 120 per day is admissible only if a student is absent from the hostel for a continuous period of not less than seven (7) consecutive days, provided written advance notice is submitted to the Mess Supervisor and Warden three days before departure.
2. Maximum mess rebate permissible in any single academic semester is twenty-one (21) days.

---

### SECTION 6: DISCIPLINE, CODE OF CONDUCT, AND ANTI-RAGGING

#### § 6.1 Zero-Tolerance Anti-Ragging Policy
1. Ragging in any form—verbal, physical, psychological, cyber, or economic—is a cognizable criminal offense under the UGC Regulations and the Madhya Pradesh Educational Institutions (Prohibition of Ragging) Act.
2. Any student found guilty of ragging shall immediately be expelled from the university hostels, debarred from academic examinations, and handed over to the local police authorities (Kishanganj Police Station, Rau).
3. The 24/7 Anti-Ragging Helpline number (1800-180-5522) and University Proctorial Board contact numbers are permanently displayed in all hostel common lobbies.

#### § 6.2 Substance Abuse, Alcohol, and Smoking
1. The consumption, possession, distribution, or facilitation of alcoholic beverages, narcotics, psychotropic substances, e-cigarettes, vapes, and tobacco products inside hostel premises is strictly prohibited.
2. Any student testing positive during random campus breathalyzer or drug screening shall face immediate termination of hostel residency, semester suspension, and forfeiture of all fee deposits.

---

### SECTION 7: VACATION, WITHDRAWAL, AND HOSTEL FEE REFUND POLICY
*(PLANTED CONTRADICTION CLAUSE 7.2)*

#### § 7.1 Room Vacation and Clearance Procedure
1. At the conclusion of the academic year or upon withdrawal, the resident must obtain a physical "No Dues Certificate" signed by the Room Inventory In-Charge, Mess Manager, and Chief Warden.
2. The student must restore all university fixtures and return keys. Any lost room key entails a lock replacement surcharge of Rs. 500.

#### § 7.2 Hostel Fee Refund on Early Withdrawal (PLANTED CONTRADICTION 3)
1. **80% Refund Within 15 Days:** If a newly admitted or continuing resident student submits a formal application for cancellation of hostel admission and vacates their room within fifteen (15) calendar days from the official date of semester commencement, **eighty percent (80%) of the total hostel accommodation fee** shall be refunded to the student's registered bank account.
2. **50% Refund Between 16 and 30 Days:** If the withdrawal notice is submitted between sixteen (16) and thirty (30) calendar days from the date of semester commencement, fifty percent (50%) of the hostel accommodation fee shall be refunded.
3. **Refund After 30 Days:** No refund of the accommodation fee shall be granted for applications received after thirty (30) calendar days from semester start.
4. **Caution Deposit Refund:** The residential security caution deposit of Rs. 10,000 is fully refundable within thirty (30) days of room vacation, after adjusting for any verified physical damage to fixtures or unpaid mess dues.

---

### SECTION 8: GRIEVANCE REDRESSAL AND HOSTEL COMMITTEE

#### § 8.1 Resident Welfare Council
1. Each hostel block shall have an elected Student Hostel Committee consisting of a Block Secretary, Mess Representative, Sports Representative, and Cultural Coordinator.
2. The committee shall convene monthly meetings with the Chief Warden and Estate Officer to review food quality, water sanitation, hygiene, and maintenance issues.
3. Grievances regarding internet connectivity, plumbing, or electrical repairs must be logged into the Medi-Caps ERP maintenance portal, with guaranteed SLA resolution within 24 hours.
