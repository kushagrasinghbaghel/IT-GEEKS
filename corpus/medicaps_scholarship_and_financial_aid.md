# MEDI-CAPS UNIVERSITY, INDORE
## INSTITUTIONAL SCHOLARSHIPS, FINANCIAL AID & CONCESSION FRAMEWORK (2024-2025)
**Document Reference:** MCU/FIN/SCHOLARSHIP/2024/02  
**Applicability:** Undergraduate, Postgraduate, and Doctoral Candidates Across All Academic Programs  
**Effective Date:** June 15, 2024  

---

### SECTION 1: OBJECTIVES AND APEX POLICY DIRECTIVES

#### § 1.1 Policy Intent and Scope
1. Medi-Caps University believes in fostering academic brilliance, promoting inclusive higher education, and rewarding exceptional athletic and scholarly merit through an institutional scholarship endowment fund.
2. Scholarships and fee concessions awarded under these regulations apply strictly to the basic Tuition Fee component. Under no circumstances are scholarships applicable to hostel charges, transportation fees, university examination fees, uniform charges, or caution deposits.
3. No student can hold more than one institutional fee concession or scholarship simultaneously. If a candidate is eligible for multiple categories, the scholarship carrying the higher monetary benefit shall be sanctioned.

---

### SECTION 2: MERIT-BASED SCHOLARSHIPS (ENTRY-LEVEL ADMISSION)

#### § 2.1 Chancellor’s Merit Scholarship Scheme
1. The Chancellor’s Merit Scholarship is awarded to freshly admitted undergraduate and postgraduate students based on qualifying competitive entrance exams or 10+2 / Diploma / Undergraduate aggregate percentages:

| Qualifying Category | Qualifying Benchmark | Tuition Fee Concession |
|:--------------------|:---------------------|:-----------------------|
| JEE (Main) Score    | 95.00 Percentile & above | 100% Tuition Fee Waiver |
| JEE (Main) Score    | 90.00 to 94.99 Percentile| 50% Tuition Fee Waiver  |
| JEE (Main) Score    | 85.00 to 89.99 Percentile| 25% Tuition Fee Waiver  |
| 10+2 CBSE/ICSE Board| 95.0% Aggregate or higher| 50% Tuition Fee Waiver  |
| 10+2 CBSE/ICSE Board| 90.0% to 94.99% Aggregate| 30% Tuition Fee Waiver  |
| 10+2 MP State Board | 90.0% Aggregate or higher| 40% Tuition Fee Waiver  |
| CAT / CMAT (for MBA)| 90.00 Percentile & above | 50% Tuition Fee Waiver  |
| GATE (for M.Tech)   | Valid GATE Score         | Rs. 6,000 / month stipend |

2. **Application Protocol:** Eligible applicants must apply through the Medi-Caps Scholarship Portal within twenty-one (21) days of admission confirmation along with verified scorecards and mark sheets.

#### § 2.2 Renewal Guidelines for Merit Scholarships
1. The Chancellor's Merit Scholarship is subject to annual academic renewal based on progressive university performance.
2. **Mandatory Renewal Standards:**
   - The recipient must maintain a minimum Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) of 8.50 at the end of each academic year (combined odd and even semester results).
   - The candidate must achieve a minimum attendance of 80% across all courses registered during the academic year.
   - The candidate must have cleared all registered courses in the first attempt with zero supplementary or backlog exams.
   - The candidate must not have incurred any disciplinary penalty, proctorial reprimand, or hostel violation.
3. If a student fails to satisfy any of the above renewal criteria in any year, the scholarship is immediately discontinued and cannot be reinstated in subsequent years.

### SECTION 3: SOCIAL, DEFENSE, AND SPECIAL WELFARE CONCESSIONS

#### § 3.1 Defense and Paramilitary Wards Concession
1. A special fee concession of ten percent (10%) on annual tuition fees is granted to dependent children of serving or retired personnel of the Indian Armed Forces (Army, Navy, Air Force) and Central Armed Police Forces (BSF, CRPF, CISF, ITBP, SSB).
2. For children of personnel martyred in the line of duty (Shaheed), a special waiver of fifty percent (50%) on annual tuition fees is granted throughout the normal duration of the degree programme upon presentation of official military gazette certification.

#### § 3.2 Sibling Fee Concession
1. In cases where two or more real biological siblings (brother/sister) are enrolled concurrently in regular degree programmes at Medi-Caps University:
   - The younger sibling shall be entitled to a fifteen percent (15%) concession on tuition fees for the semesters during which both siblings are simultaneously enrolled.
   - The concession terminates automatically upon the graduation or withdrawal of the elder sibling.
2. Submission of joint birth certificates, Aadhaar cards, and parents' affidavits is mandatory to substantiate sibling claims.

#### § 3.3 Beti Bachao - Single Girl Child Assistance
1. To encourage higher professional education for women, female students who are the only child of their parents (single girl child with no surviving brothers or sisters) are entitled to an annual tuition fee concession of twenty percent (20%).
2. The claim must be supported by an executive magistrate affidavit affirming single girl child status.

### SECTION 4: SPORTS EXCELLENCE AND ATHLETIC SCHOLARSHIPS

#### § 4.1 Athletic Representation Slabs
Students who have represented the state or nation in recognized sports competitions (under the Indian Olympic Association or AIU) are entitled to sports fee waivers:
- **International Representation:** 100% Tuition Fee Waiver and complimentary on-campus hostel accommodation.
- **National Medalist (Gold/Silver/Bronze at SGFI or Senior Nationals):** 50% Tuition Fee Waiver.
- **State Level Gold Medalist:** 25% Tuition Fee Waiver.

#### § 4.2 Ongoing Athletic Obligations
1. All sports scholarship holders must actively participate in university trials, coaching camps, and represent Medi-Caps University in all inter-collegiate tournaments.
2. Inability to participate due to unapproved absence or refusal to attend mandatory sports training shall result in immediate revoking of scholarship benefits.

---

### SECTION 5: TEACHING AND RESEARCH ASSISTANTSHIPS (PG & PH.D.)

#### § 5.1 Graduate Teaching Assistantships (GTA)
1. Regular full-time postgraduate students in M.Tech, M.Pharm, and MCA may be awarded Graduate Teaching Assistantships of Rs. 8,000 per month.
2. In exchange for the financial stipend, GTA scholars must deliver eight (8) hours per week of laboratory practical assistance, assignment grading, or tutorial mentoring under departmental supervision.

#### § 5.2 Doctoral Fellowship
1. Full-time PhD scholars enrolled in university teaching departments without CSIR/UGC-JRF funding are eligible for Medi-Caps University Doctoral Research Fellowships of Rs. 20,000 per month for the first two years, extendable to Rs. 25,000 per month in the third year upon successful comprehensive evaluation.
2. Fellowship awards are contingent upon semester-by-semester progress verification by the Departmental Research Committee (DRC).

---

### SECTION 6: DISQUALIFICATION, FORFEITURE AND RECOVERY

#### § 6.1 Forfeiture Due to Academic Dishonesty or Disciplinary Indiscipline
1. If any scholarship recipient is found guilty of resorting to unfair means (UFM) in internal or end-semester examinations, the scholarship shall be revoked with retroactive effect from the beginning of that academic semester.
2. Any act of ragging, vandalism, campus violence, substance abuse, or unauthorized political demonstration leads to automatic permanent disqualification from all financial aid programmes.
3. If a student obtains a scholarship by producing forged documents, deceptive domicile records, or falsified mark sheets, the University shall institute criminal proceedings and recover all disbursed benefits with an 18% per annum penal interest.
